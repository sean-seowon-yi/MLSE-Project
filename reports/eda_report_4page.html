Exploratory Data Analysis: Player Similarity Features¶

This analysis identifies discriminative features for player similarity from SkillCorner tracking data and StatsBomb event data.

Scope: 10 SkillCorner matches (237 players), 3,464 StatsBomb matches sampled for action profiles.

Section 1: Data Overview¶

SkillCorner: Continuous (x, y) positions at 10 frames/second. Coordinate system: meters with pitch center as origin.

StatsBomb: Discrete event-by-event records of on-ball actions. Coordinate system: 120×80 unit grid, bottom-left origin.
